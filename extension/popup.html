How this works
  1. Open a chapter on any manga site.
  2. Click Read this chapter.
  3. Say yes when your browser asks about the image server.
  4. It opens in Couchlist — no ads, your own settings.

Your browser asks once per image server, then remembers. Couchlist never sees the pages, and this cannot get past a login or a CAPTCHA.
